How it works

  1. Hold an Agent ID in your wallet (Agent IDs are claimed inside the Binance app).
  2. Connect your wallet here — we only ever use the standard injected wallet connection.
  3. Press Check In Today and confirm in your wallet. One check-in per UTC day.
  4. Come back tomorrow to grow your streak. Miss a full day and the streak restarts at 1.
  5. At 3, 7, 14, and 30-day streaks, claim your soulbound badge — free, forever yours.

Check-ins cost only the BNB Smart Chain network gas fee, paid to the network — Agent Roll Call never charges a fee, never takes custody of funds, and the contract cannot move your assets. Badges are collectibles that record your streak; no monetary value is promised or implied.

Agent Roll Call is a community project built for the Agent ID Hackathon. Agent ID is checked on-chain via the public Agent ID registry contract — your address is only ever read, never tracked off-chain.
